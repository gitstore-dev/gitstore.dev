02 / PLAIN FILES. SERIOUS POSSIBILITIES.

Describe the catalog.
Let the engine work.

Products, variants, categories, and collections live in Markdown with structured YAML frontmatter. Your tools edit files. Git records intent. GitStore validates and reconciles admitted resources into queryable catalog state.

01

Your existing Git tools

Clone, commit, and push over Smart HTTP. Connect the editors and automation your team already uses.

02

Validation before projection

Pre-receive checks catch structural errors. Admission checks govern what becomes available through GraphQL.

03

Reconciliation, with visible status

Controllers work toward the declared state and report conditions. A push is the start of processing, not a promise of instant publication.

Keep your team in control.
